Personal rclone · Updated October 15, 2026

Privacy Policy

This policy describes Personal rclone, an OAuth application operated by Luotanw solely for the owner's personal Google Drive use.

Data accessed and purpose

When authorized by the owner, rclone can access Google Drive file contents and metadata, including names, folders, sizes, and modification times. Access is used to list, copy, back up, and synchronize files. Uploads, changes, and deletions occur according to the owner's commands and settings.

Storage and retention

Rclone stores authorization tokens in its configuration on the machine where it runs. File copies and operational logs may be stored on the owner's devices or at storage destinations the owner configures. Retention depends on those settings; the owner controls deletion of stored copies, logs, and credentials.

Transfers and sharing

File data is transferred between Google Drive and destinations selected by the owner. Google and any configured storage provider process data under their own applicable terms and privacy policies. The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke authorization through Google Account connections and remove saved credentials from the rclone configuration. Revoking authorization prevents future access through that authorization; it does not erase existing copies. The owner can delete those copies from the devices and storage destinations where they were saved.

This website

These informational pages do not request Google credentials or receive Drive files. The pages contain no analytics scripts. GitHub hosts the website and may process ordinary website request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact and changes

Operator: Luotanw on GitHub. For questions, use the support email identified on the application's Google authorization screen. This policy will be updated if the setup's data practices change.
