Personal rclone

A personal Google Drive connection used by Luotanw with rclone to copy, back up, and synchronize the owner's files.

Why Google Drive access is requested

The connection allows rclone to list and read files, upload or update files, and delete files when the owner runs commands that require those actions. Operations and destinations are chosen by the owner.

Who uses this connection

This OAuth application is intended solely for the owner's personal use. This website provides information about the connection; it does not provide a public file-transfer service.

Privacy and contact

Read the Privacy Policy for details about data access, storage, and revoking access.

Operator: Luotanw on GitHub. The Google authorization screen also identifies the support email for this application.
